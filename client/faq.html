FAQ

Straight answers.

What Veyrion is, what it is not and how to get in touch.

Who is behind Veyrion?

Marcu Brasovean. Veyrion is currently a one-person software project currently developed by Marcu, not a 12-person engineering agency.

When did the idea start?

The concept began in 2025. The website and its backend are being developed as the project grows.

What kind of work is shown here?

The site focuses on web development, backend and API work, automation, application security, FTC robotics and game-development experiments.

Are the projects client case studies?

No. The Work page shows personal and team projects. It does not present fictional clients, invented endorsements or unverified commercial results.

Can I request a project?

Yes. Use the contact page to explain what you want to build. Availability and scope are discussed individually; no fixed response time or delivery date is promised.

Is Veyrion hiring?

No. The project is currently led by Marcu alone. The collaboration page is for ideas and possible collaboration, not job openings.

Still have a question? Contact Marcu.
