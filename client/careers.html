CULTURE

No meetings before noon

Mornings are for deep work. Meetings happen in the afternoon when your brain can handle them.

Ship to production weekly

We deploy early and often. Staging environments are a lie. Real traffic is the only test that matters.

Write things down

Design docs, ADRs, architecture decision records. If you can't find it in writing, it didn't happen.

No on-call rotation

We build systems that don't need on-call. If something breaks at 3 a.m., we failed at design time.
