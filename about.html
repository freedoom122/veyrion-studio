The studio

Small team. Every person has shipped production systems at scale. We like it that way.

How this started

Veyrion started in 2019 because two engineers kept getting asked the same question: "Can you just look at this? Nobody else can figure it out."

The problems were always the same shape. Systems that had outgrown their original architecture. Pipelines that broke every Tuesday. Databases that nobody dared migrate. The kind of work where "just use a managed service" stopped being an option.

We said yes enough times that it became a studio. No pitch decks. No sales team. No growth hacks. Just engineers who'd rather fix the problem than talk about fixing it.

Six years later, we're still small on purpose. Every person at Veyrion has shipped production systems that handle real traffic, real money, and real consequences when they break. We don't hire for potential. We hire for proof.

How we work

No billable hours

Fixed-scope engagements. We quote a number, you know the budget. Nobody's watching a clock.

No NDAs for existing clients

If you want to see our work, we'll show you. We're proud of what we've built and we think transparency builds trust.

Asynchronous by default

We write things down. Design docs, ADRs, architecture decision records. If you can't find it in writing, it didn't happen.

Production from week one

We deploy early and often. Staging environments are a lie. Real traffic is the only test that matters.
